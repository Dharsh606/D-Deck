QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Straight answers

Everything you need to know about D-Deck.

Yes, completely. D-Deck has no paywalls, no trial periods, and no subscriptions. It is licensed under the open-source MIT License and will remain free forever.
All your tasks, routines, settings, and streak metrics are stored locally on your PC in %APPDATA%/d-deck/d-deck-data.json. Zero bytes are ever transmitted to any remote servers.
D-Deck is designed as a true desktop HUD companion. Keeping it off the Windows taskbar and out of Alt+Tab ensures your multitasking space remains pristine. You can summon or hide it anytime with Ctrl+Shift+D or via the system tray.
When you set a task with a scheduled time, D-Deck alerts you 1 minute before time, at the scheduled minute, and then persists with a subtle alert every minute until you check it off.
The Android APK companion is currently in development. It will feature homescreen widgets, focus sprint synchronizing, and local Wi-Fi pairing. Star the repository on GitHub to receive instant release notifications!
